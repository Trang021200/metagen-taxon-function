## What we did

- Downloaded and verified the data (`wget`, `md5sum`)
- Prepared samplesheets, database sheets, `params.json` and a launch script
- Ran QC and taxonomic profiling with nf-core/taxprofiler
- Ran functional profiling with nf-core/funcprofiler
- Imported and explored the results in R with mia and miaViz
